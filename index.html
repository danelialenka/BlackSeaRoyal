Refined coastal hospitality

A distinctive stay in the heart of Kobuleti

Black Sea Royal Hotel combines a striking black-and-gold identity with friendly, personal service. It is an ideal base for beach holidays, weekend escapes and business travel.

Relax after a day on the coast, enjoy local dining nearby and experience the easy pace of one of Georgia's best-known seaside towns.

Location Kobuleti, Adjara, Georgia
